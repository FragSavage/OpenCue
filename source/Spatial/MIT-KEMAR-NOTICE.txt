The four files under data/ are unmodified 44.1-kHz stereo PCM16 WAVs from the
official compact archive, elevation 0 degrees and azimuths 0, 30, 90, 150 degrees.
kemar_hrir.h contains the same signed sample values, generated losslessly by
generate_hrir.ps1. Negative source azimuths swap the measured left/right ears.
OpenCue's original renderer resamples these coefficients to the endpoint rate.

Only the measurement data are used. No MIT demonstration spatializer code,
proprietary Rara DSP, ArtTune DSP, or paid preset is included in OpenCue Spatial.
